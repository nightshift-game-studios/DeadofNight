# Drowned Cannery: credits

Every asset is **CC0 1.0** (public domain). Nothing is CC-BY, NC or of unclear license. Shared files are **referenced in
place** (not copied or moved), so they cost no extra disk. Only the procedural decals below are new files.

## New in this map: `assets/decals/` (CC0 1.0, made for this project)
Generated by procedural noise + DejaVu Sans text, 512 px or smaller: oil_stain, grime_streak, hazard_stripe, platform_edge,
blood_smear and the room signs sign_pier, sign_netloft, sign_icehouse, sign_smokehouse, sign_quay, sign_slipway, sign_boiler,
sign_retort, sign_tanks. (Sign text is rasterised DejaVu Sans; its license permits this and no font file ships.)
